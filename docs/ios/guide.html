Install HVI CRM
on your iPhone

Add the HVI CRM to your Home Screen so it opens like a regular app: full screen, with its own icon, in a few taps. It takes about one minute and works on any iPhone.

https://highvoltageindustries-crm.netlify.app

You need: an iPhone with a recent iOS, the Safari app, and your High Voltage Industries Google account.

Steps 1 & 2 · Open it in Safari and tap the menu

Use Safari (the blue compass icon). Illustrations follow the latest iPhone and Safari design.

1Open the HVI CRM link in Safari

  • Open Safari and go to:
    highvoltageindustries-crm.netlify.app
  • Sign in once with Continue with Google using your High Voltage Industries account.

2Tap the menu button in the bottom bar

  • Look at the bar at the bottom of the screen. Next to the page address there is a menu button (three lines or three dots, depending on your iOS version), marked in red.
  • Tap it. A menu opens above the bar.
  • If the bottom bar is hidden, tap the very bottom edge of the screen or scroll up a little.
Tip: if you opened the link from Gmail, Slack or another app, first tap Open in Safari. The Add to Home Screen option only appears in Safari itself.

3Tap “Share”

  • In the menu that opened, tap Share (square with an arrow pointing up).

4Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”

  • Swipe the share sheet up until you see Add to Home Screen (square with a plus sign). On an iPhone set to Portuguese it reads Adicionar à Tela de Início.
  • Tap it.
Cannot find it? Scroll to the very bottom of the share sheet and tap Edit Actions…, then add Add to Home Screen. Make sure you are in Safari and not in another app's built-in browser.

5Check the name and tap “Add”

  • The name is pre-filled as HVI CRM. You can change it if you like.
  • Tap Add in the top-right corner.

6Open HVI CRM from your Home Screen

  • Find the black HVI CRM icon (it may be on your last Home Screen page).
  • Tap it: the CRM opens full screen, without the Safari address bar, like an app.
  • Tip: press and hold the icon and drag it to your first page or into your Dock.

Tips & troubleshooting

Things the team asked about most often.

QuestionAnswer
I am asked to sign in againUse Continue with Google with your High Voltage Industries account. You may need to sign in once inside the app; after that it remembers you.
Will I get updates?Yes, automatically. The app always loads the latest version of the CRM; there is nothing to update in the App Store.
Is data synced with my computer?Yes. It is the same CRM and the same data, only in a phone-friendly layout.
Dark modeOpen the menu (☰ at the top-left of the CRM) and tap the sun/moon button to switch between light and dark.
Where is the CRM menu?Tap the ☰ button at the top-left of the CRM to open the navigation (Pipeline, Leads, Projects, DTC, and more).
The option is missingOpen the link in Safari (not inside Gmail, Slack or another app) and make sure your iPhone is up to date.
How do I remove it?Press and hold the icon, tap Remove App, then Delete from Home Screen. Your data stays in the CRM.
Using Chrome on iPhoneRecent iPhone versions can also do this from Chrome (Share → Add to Home Screen), but Safari is recommended.
