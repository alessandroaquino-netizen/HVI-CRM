Install HVI CRM
on your iPhone

Add the HVI CRM to your Home Screen so it opens like a regular app: full screen, with its own icon, in a few taps. It takes about one minute and works on any iPhone.

HVI CRM link: ________________________________

You need: an iPhone with iOS 16.4 or newer, the Safari app, and your High Voltage Industries Google account.

Step 1 & 2 · Open it in Safari and tap Share

Use Safari (the blue compass). On iPhone, Safari is the most reliable browser for this.

1Open the HVI CRM link in Safari

  • Open Safari on your iPhone.
  • Type or paste the HVI CRM link in the address bar and go.
  • Sign in once with Continue with Google using your High Voltage Industries account.

2Tap the Share button

  • It is the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bar at the bottom of the screen (marked in red on the screenshot).
  • If you do not see the bottom bar, tap once at the bottom edge of the screen or scroll up slightly.
Tip: if you open the link from an email or chat app, tap the compass / “Open in Safari” icon first. The Add to Home Screen option only appears in the browser itself.

3Scroll the menu and tap “Add to Home Screen”

  • Swipe the list up until you see Add to Home Screen (a square with a plus sign).
  • Tap it.
Cannot find it? Scroll all the way down and tap Edit Actions…, then add Add to Home Screen to your list. Also make sure you are in Safari and not inside another app's built-in browser.

4Check the name and tap “Add”

  • The name is pre-filled as HVI CRM. You can change it if you like.
  • Tap Add in the top-right corner.

5Open HVI CRM from your Home Screen

  • Find the black HVI CRM icon (it may be on your last Home Screen page).
  • Tap it: the CRM opens full screen, without the Safari address bar, like an app.
  • Tip: press and hold the icon and drag it to your first page or into your Dock for one-tap access.

Tips & troubleshooting

Things the team asked about most often.

QuestionAnswer
I am asked to sign in againUse Continue with Google with your High Voltage Industries account. If you were signed in on Safari before installing, you may need to sign in once inside the app. After that it remembers you.
Will I get updates?Yes, automatically. The app always loads the latest version of the CRM; there is nothing to update in the App Store.
Is data synced with my computer?Yes. It is the same CRM and the same data, only in a phone-friendly layout.
Dark modeTap the sun/moon button next to your name in the menu (☰ at the top-left) to switch between light and dark.
Where is the menu?Tap the ☰ button at the top-left to open the navigation (Pipeline, Leads, Projects, DTC, and more).
The option is missingOpen the link in Safari (not inside Gmail, Slack or another app). Update iPhone to iOS 16.4 or newer if the menu still does not show it.
How do I remove it?Press and hold the icon, tap Remove App, then Delete from Home Screen. Your data stays in the CRM.
Using Chrome on iPhoneRecent iPhone versions can also do this from Chrome (Share → Add to Home Screen), but Safari is recommended.
Need help? Contact Alessandro Aquino and include a screenshot of where you got stuck.
